# Community
r/TheLastAirbender (public). It covers two shows (ATLA and Korra) in one franchise.
Chosen despite the one-show guideline because both ended years ago (2008 and 2014),
so spoiler risk is minimal. I only use posts about the original ATLA/Korra, nothing
about the live-action series or new/upcoming releases.

# Distinctions I noticed
- Some posts back a claim with a specific episode, scene, or quote. Most don't.
- Many posts are strong opinions ("Zuko's arc is the best ever") with no support.
- Some are jokes, memes, or quick reactions, not arguments.

# Labels
- argued: makes a claim about the show and backs it with a specific, checkable
  detail (an episode, scene, quote, or event).
  - Example 1: "Jet is overhated. He's literally a depiction of what happened in real life and how extremist come to exist. His whole town was killed and he's one of the view survivors. He literally witnessed his family and friends being killed by the fire nation... And at the end with all the hate he had towards the fire nation it wasn't even the fire nation that killed him."
  - Example 2: "Unpopular Opinion: Zuko and Ozai have a lot of parallels... Both are disfavored by their fathers. Both have a more talented and smarter sibling that their father favors over them... Both end up betraying their fathers. With Ozai, it was Azulon. With Zuko, it was both Ozai and Iroh."
- take: a confident opinion, ranking, or reaction with no specific evidence offered.
  - Example 1: "I enjoyed legend of Korra just as much as I enjoyed TLA... I don't understand why there's so much hate it's just a different story in the same universe"
  - Example 2: "It's ok to not enjoy season 3 compared to the other seasons :|"

# Decision rule (hardest boundary: argued vs take)
If the post names a specific episode, scene, quote, or in-show event as a reason
for its opinion, it's argued, even if it's short or heated. Just mentioning a
character's name doesn't count.
